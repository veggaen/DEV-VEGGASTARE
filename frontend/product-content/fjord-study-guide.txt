SET UP YOUR WALLPAPER

its wallpaper action. Review the crop before applying it.
Menu names can vary by operating-system version.

CHOOSING A CROP
Fit keeps the whole image, but may add margins on differently shaped
screens. Fill covers the screen and crops the edges. On a portrait phone,
move the crop to keep your preferred building or mountain in view.
For ultrawide displays, Fit is recommended to avoid a heavy crop.
Large displays may soften the image when it is enlarged beyond 1536 pixels.
