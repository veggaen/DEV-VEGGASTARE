## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished downloadable digital artwork for the Veggat Interview Pack, also used in a product gallery. Primary request: create one original premium landscape digital artwork inspired by the calm geometry of Norwegian fjords, with layered sculptural stone forms, deep emerald water, pale mineral surfaces, and soft northern light. Style: meticulously rendered architectural still-life / abstract landscape, tactile stone and glass, refined editorial art direction, crisp detail with restrained grain, beautiful balanced negative space. This is the actual artwork, not a product mockup, not a webpage screenshot, not framed on a wall. Wide landscape composition, polished at full resolution. Constraints: no text, no logo, no watermark, no people, no UI, no recognizable copyrighted character. An honest small digital art download for a software portfolio showcase.

The imagegen skill supplied the generation/inspection workflow; Sharp only performs format conversion and bounded preview resizing. No payment or delivery claim is made until S4 verifies fulfillment.
